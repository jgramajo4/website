A curated dataset of all Nouns DAO proposals, extracted from onchain data and structured for ML training. Each record includes the proposal title, full description, final vote outcome (pass/fail), proposer address, and relevant metadata.

Built as the foundation for governance analytics and NLP experiments in the DAO space. The dataset is split into train/test sets (stratified by outcome) and ships with a model card documenting the schema and known limitations. Used to train both the [proposal predictor](https://huggingface.co/gramajo/nouns-proposal-predictor) and the [compact nouns-model](https://huggingface.co/gramajo/nouns-model).

A DistilBERT-based text classifier fine-tuned on every Nouns DAO proposal to date (983 proposals). The model ingests a proposal's title and description and predicts whether it's likely to pass or fail — no token holdings, no voter history, just the words.

Built as an experiment in making DAO governance more legible through NLP. The training pipeline uses the [nouns-proposals dataset](https://huggingface.co/gramajo/nouns-proposals) with Weights & Biases for experiment tracking. Serves as a benchmark for how well language alone captures the signals that drive onchain voting.

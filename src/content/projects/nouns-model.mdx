A compact DistilBERT model (67M parameters) trained to classify Nouns DAO proposals as pass or fail from their text alone. Built for speed and accessibility — inference runs comfortably on CPU, no GPU required.

This is the production-ready variant, trimmed and optimized from the research training pipeline. Pairs with the [nouns-proposals dataset](https://huggingface.co/gramajo/nouns-proposals) and can be loaded in a few lines with the `transformers` library.
